## New
- Notifications show who sent them with their photo, and a chat's latest messages. Reply or mark it read right from the notification.
- A photo in an announcement or channel post shows large in its notification, and results come with a Standings button.
- A bell on Home opens every notification this phone has shown, filtered by kind, so one you swiped away can be found again.
- Account → Notifications: switch each kind on or off (chats, announcements and channels, results and reminders), check that CTR[L]APS can run in the background, and send yourself a test notification of each kind.
- Theme: light, dark or as the phone is; a pure black page; a colour theme (gold, orange, green, blue or violet), Material You, or your own colour picked on a colour wheel; and the phone's own font if you prefer it.
- Search settings at the top of Account finds any setting and opens it.
- Your QR code opens from the button beside the search, with Verify someone in the same sheet.
- Tap any photo and it grows into view. Team, group and weekend photos are changed or removed right there by those who may; with no photo, they pick one straight away.
- Race weekends can have a track photo at the top of the weekend's page, fading into Home's next-race card.
- Choose which notifications pop up: chats, announcements, channel posts and results. Urgent messages always come.
- Reminders before each session ("Qualifying starts in 15 minutes"), on time even when the phone sleeps: choose how long before, and your sessions or all of them.
- Home-screen widgets: the next session with a countdown, your unread chats, and the top 5 of your category.
- A countdown to the next session on Home and on each race weekend.
- Pull down to refresh Home, Chats, Schedule, Standings and People.
- Every team has its own page: its photo, where it stands in each category, its people and its latest results. Open it from Teams, Standings or a person's team.
- [admin, coordinator, team_manager] Add a photo for a team, such as its logo or car.
- [admin, coordinator] When you add or promote someone as a delegate, pick their delegation, and they join its group chat at once.
- Standings open with a podium for the top three, with drivers' and teams' photos and each one's points shown as a bar against the leader.
- [admin] Name a session on two wheels: its kind (Practice, Qualifying, Race…) and its number, which picks the next free one; a name the weekend already has asks before saving.
- [admin] Delete a session from its edit window.
- [admin] Add, change or remove a weekend's track photo, cropped to the header with a preview before it uploads.
- About shows the people behind CTR[L]APS, and Terms, Privacy Policy, the License and FAQ answers open in sheets.
- Updates can be skipped one version at a time, and a new version brings a notification.
- The website has a front page showing what CTR[L]APS does and where to get the app; signing in is at /login.

## Improved
- Messages from the app now show in a bar above the tabs; deleting a message, removing a session or a photo, or clearing your notifications can be undone from it.
- Pages show grey placeholders while they load, and empty pages say what's missing and what you can do next.
- The website has a download page with the latest version, what's new and a QR code to open it on your phone.
- Account and its pages are laid out like the phone's own settings: a profile banner, rows with icons, and large titles.
- A person's page opens with their banner and quick actions (Message, Star, QR code, Edit), with their details below.
- The Schedule shows each weekend's days at a glance, and the track's own time only when it differs from yours.
- People are listed by role without boxes, with each person's team and race categories under their name; a status shows only when it isn't Active.
- [admin, coordinator] Teams: tap categories and rename teams, then save them all at once from the bar at the bottom; any that fail stay for another try.
- [admin, coordinator] Change role opens in its own sheet on a person's page.
- Permissions shows at a glance whether everything is allowed, with a switch for each and what stops working when one is off.
- Permissions opens Xiaomi's battery saver and the autostart page on many phone makers, and says when a kind of notification is switched off.
- Updates download only what your phone needs, about 60% smaller.
- The app and the website use one font, Plus Jakarta Sans, and the website follows the same theme choices as the app.
- Category tags are easier to read, and status tags no longer spread their letters out.
- The category chips on Schedule and Standings show how many weekends or results each one has.
- Signing out asks first, in a sheet; tick "Don't ask me again" to skip it, and Settings brings the question back.
- [admin, coordinator] When you give someone a role, pick their team from a list as you type; a name that isn't a team yet warns before a new one is made.
- [admin, coordinator] The Volunteers and Security groups on People have an Email link.
- Updates are checked before they install: a damaged download is refused, so try again.
- On Android 12 and later, what is behind a dialog is blurred.
- The launch screen shows the championship badge larger and sharp, with nothing cut off.
