## New
- Groups: create a group, invite people, choose admins and who may send, and leave when you want.
- Race weekend channels: Chats | Channels, season by season.
- Select messages to reply, edit, copy, delete or forward them; forwarded messages are marked.
- Message info: who your message reached and who read it.
- Search a chat, and export it as a file.
- A popup shows new messages while the app is open.
- Updates install without asking each time, and What's new shows after an update.
- [admin] Open or close a channel, name channel managers, and make a season current.

## Improved
- Tap a chat's header to see the person's ID card.
- Pull the chat list down for All, Unread and Groups.
- Forwarding and deleting show at once.
- Race weekend sessions fold away under an arrow.
