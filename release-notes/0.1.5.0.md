## New
- Send up to 30 photos at once, plus documents and audio; photos sent together show as one grid.
- A photo editor before sending: crop, rotate, draw, add text, a caption for each photo, and HD.
- A new attach sheet with your recent photos, and a Files page for documents.
- Polls and events in groups and announcements, with reminders and Upcoming events on Home.
- Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and monospace in messages, shown as you type.
- Link previews.
- Save photos, documents and audio to your phone.
- Account is now a menu: Account, Storage, Settings and About.
- Settings shows each permission the app needs and whether it is allowed.

## Improved
- Photos are made smaller before sending, so they send and arrive faster.
- Screens slide smoothly and open faster.
- Any kind of file can be sent as a document.
