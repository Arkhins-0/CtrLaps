## New
- Settings → Interface: switch animations off or choose how quick they are, switch the light buzz on or off, and turn blur off behind dialogs and on your profile banner.
- A light buzz when you flip a switch, star someone, mute a channel, send a message, pick a chip, turn a session wheel or pull a page to refresh.
- If CTR[L]APS ever stops, a page says so and shows what happened: raise a support ticket with the report filled in and attached, copy it, share it, or open the app again.
- Add screenshots and files to a support ticket.
- Long-press the version at the bottom of Account or About to copy your phone's details for a support ticket.
- Long-press your photo in the tab bar to open your details.

## Improved
- Home's cards, your chats and People slide in one after another as the tab opens.
- Stars and the mute bell pop as they switch, their colour fading across.
- Sections open and close with one smooth slide: a driver's sessions in Standings and a weekend's sessions.
- [admin, coordinator] The Today card and its lists open and close smoothly, and so does a row in the results editor.
- The launch screen slides up and away as the app opens.
- A support ticket reads like a chat: your messages and Support's in bubbles, with what it's about at the top and its details shown once.
- Messages sent very quickly one after another wait a moment and then go by themselves, instead of failing.

## Fixed
- An announcement, channel post, email or support ticket sent twice by a double tap or a dropped connection now goes out only once.
- [admin, coordinator] Results notices go out once, even when two people save the same results at the same moment.
