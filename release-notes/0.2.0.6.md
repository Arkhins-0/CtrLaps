## New
- Mute a race weekend's or a category's channel with the bell: no notifications from it, and its unread count shows as an outline.
- Every category channel is open to everyone.
- [coordinator] Change a session's start and end times on the schedule; everyone gets the update.

- [admin, coordinator, volunteer] Volunteer groups: each has a name, a lead coordinator and its own group chat on a new Volunteers page in Chats.
- [admin, coordinator] Hand a volunteer group to another coordinator in one step; its volunteers and chat go with it.
- [admin, coordinator] In a volunteer group's chat, let a volunteer chat, stop them sending, or make them read only.
- Race officials are now called Delegates.
- [admin, coordinator, delegate] Delegations: put each delegate in a delegation such as a sponsor or the federation; each has its own group chat with every admin and coordinator in it.
- [admin, coordinator] Move delegates between delegations, one or many at a time, and let each one chat, stop them sending, or make them read only.
- [delegate, volunteer] Your delegation or volunteer group is pinned at the top of Chats.
- [admin, coordinator] A Today card on Home: today's sessions, time changes, and people who haven't accepted their invite, finished their profile, or are suspended.

- A red NEW line under the last unread post in announcements and every channel, so you can see where the new posts end.
- Announcements and channels are split by day, with the day's heading staying at the top while you scroll; each post shows just its time.

## Improved
- [admin, coordinator] Send an announcement to anyone, fans included.
- [admin] Pick the coordinators who manage a race weekend's or a category's channel; they post there.
- [coordinator] Post in the weekend and category channels an admin makes you a manager of.
- [coordinator] Look after every team manager, racer, crew member, race official, security member, volunteer and user, not only the people you added: their status, profile, team and role.
- [admin, coordinator] Only admins can make someone a coordinator now.
- [coordinator] Change your own name, contact, date of birth, photo and email from Account.
- The Account tab shows the organisation's website next to the app version.
- [delegate] Private chats are with admins, coordinators and team managers; group chat happens in your delegation.
- [admin, coordinator] Only admins and coordinators enter results now.
