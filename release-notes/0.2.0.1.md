## New
- Anyone can create an account with their email; an organiser then gives them a role.
- Themes: light, dark, or follow the phone (dark to start).
- Automatic downloads can be switched off for photos, voice notes and documents; a photo then shows blurred until you tap it.
- Verify remembers the people you checked, and you can star people to keep them at the top.
- A copy button beside your account code.
- [user] Edit your own name, contact, date of birth and photo, and change your email.
- [admin, coordinator, team_manager, security_head] Promote someone, or change their role, from their page.

## Improved
- Profile photos are cropped square.
- Storage shows what the app keeps and how full your phone is.
- Signing out asks first.
- A show-password eye in every password box.
- Race officials can chat and make groups.
